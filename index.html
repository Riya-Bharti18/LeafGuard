🔍 Visual Symptoms & Interpretation

Concentric brown rings ("target-board" spots) surrounded by yellow chlorotic halos, typically beginning on older lower leaves. Caused by the fungal pathogen Alternaria solani.

🧠 Grad-CAM Explainability
🍃
Attention/activation heatmap: Red/amber zones contributed most to the prediction. Not biological proof.
🌱 Practical Next Steps (Non-Chemical First)
  • 1
    Immediately remove and safely destroy severely infected lower leaves. Do not compost them.
  • 2
    Avoid overhead irrigation; water at the base of the plant to keep foliage dry.
  • 3
    Apply organic mulch (straw or plastic) around the stem to prevent soil splashback.
  • 4
    Ensure at least 60 cm spacing between plants for adequate air circulation.
Source: ICAR - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR) · Verified Guide (2025)

Known Limitations & Risks
Dataset Bias: PlantVillage images were captured under controlled laboratory lighting. Field-condition accuracy may differ.
Abstention Policy: Predictions below 60% confidence are marked "Inconclusive" to prevent false certainty.
No Chemical Prescriptions: Only general cultural and non-chemical practices are suggested without extension officer sign-off.
